Lock: keep the target inside the pipper ring until the tone goes solid. IR seekers are fooled by flares, radar missiles by chaff. Throttle gauge: right of the radar (yellow tick = actual speed). Power (4/5/6/7): 12 points over weapons / shields / engines; engines also size the afterburner pool. Newtonian mode (F) adds velocity / retrograde / relative-velocity markers. Graphics: P cycles low / mid / high / ultra, O has device presets and distance LOD; add ?q=high or ?q=ultra to the URL for antialiasing and a larger sky.
v0.2 feel: white ticks round the reticle = your shots landed (blue shield, grey armour, orange subsystem, red kill); a red arc round the reticle = fire coming from that side; CHAIN x2+ multiplies kill score, keep killing inside the timer. Salvage pods (diamonds on the radar and HUD): fly through them, S shield · R hull · M missiles · A ammo · O overdrive; nothing to press, touch the same. Gauntlet (main menu): after each wave press 1 / 2 / 3 (touch: tap a card) to take a perk; the AI ignores all of this. Flight: F cycles ARCADE / SIM (TIE Fighter style: speed follows the throttle at real acceleration, the velocity marker trails the nose in a hard turn) / NEWTONIAN; BACKSPACE (touch: double-tap the throttle slider) matches the target's speed; OPTIONS has "sim purist" to switch off markers, banners, pods and hit-stop.
