Shapes keep their voxel detail with softened edges. Same triangles as the merged cubes; spawn costs more CPU (Bullfrog 107 vs 64 ms), hits rebuild only the touched slabs within about 4 ms per frame. `?vox=cube` and `?vox=inst` show the earlier renderers; `?vsm=1|2` rounder smoothing and `?vdrop=1` one detail step lower (half the triangles; judged too low in detail).
