# Ejection outcomes and presentation

The roster and ejection cutscenes default to enabled (`tuning.roster.enabled` and `tuning.cutscenes.enabled`). Turn either flag off in the database to revert that system; `?nocut` skips all ejection sequences. A capsule with a reachable friendly rescuer can still be picked up in play. An actively threatened rescuer or a SAR ship already routing to a closer capsule does not delay the outcome beat. Otherwise, after the configurable 12-second game-time beat, the shared seeded `rescueOutcomeRoll` weighs living forces, rescue ships, a SAR ship routing to the capsule and hostile proximity. Space or the touch button resolves the beat early. No contacts can leave a pilot missing. AI pilots use the same policy at mission end.

Recovered pilots enter RECOVERING; captured pilots enter CAPTURED, and a pending entry in the saved roster's `capturePending` list is a hook for a future scripted consequence. No rescue mission is created by this change. Existing award rules handle rescue and survival of capture when the relevant events occur.

Ejection, recovery, capture and missing-pilot scenes use `assets/scenes/ejection/shots.json`. Repeat viewings are skipped using local storage, and Escape, Enter, Space or tap skips the scene. Reduced-motion disables the pan. Existing proposal sheets supply the frames and portraits: **zero new image files or image bytes** were added. These are prototype storyboard crops, not final production illustrations; character continuity and visual finish need owner review. No queen or egg sac is used. The shot list and images are served from `assets/` alongside the built HTML.

Scripted legacy callsign inventory: HAWK and SPARROW denote wing slots, ECHO and MEOW the player. References occur in `src/55_ships.js`, `src/73_roster.js`, `src/74_survival.js`, `src/75_mission.js`, `src/76_menu.js`, `src/77_retreat.js`, `src/78_coms.js`, and `src/81_scene.js`. With roster on, mission text resolves these slots to assigned live pilots and suppresses absent speakers; queued actor speech is dropped if the actor dies. Legacy strings remain for authored mission data and flag-off compatibility.
