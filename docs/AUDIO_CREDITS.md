# Audio credits and how the audio is built

All audio currently in the game is **CC0 (public domain dedication)**; no attribution is legally required, credits are given anyway.
Licence statements were taken from each source page on 2026-10-21 (see the URLs); `docs/AUDIO_SOURCES.md` holds the wider research and the shortlist.

### Original menu cue

`audio/menu-frontier.mp3` is **Frontier Overture**, an original 92 BPM D-minor
strings, horn, piano and synth-bass menu cue composed for this project on
2026-11-01. Its reproducible hand-written MIDI source and generator are
`tools/gen/music/generate-menu-frontier.mjs` and
`tools/gen/music/menu-frontier-overture.mid`. It contains no sampled material
or melody from Wing Commander or any other game. It was rendered offline by
`tools/gen/music/render-menu-frontier.mjs`, using only additive oscillator
synthesis (no soundfont or samples). The file is played only after the game's existing first tap/key
audio unlock and remains governed by the Music option and volume slider.

## Sound samples (embedded as base64 in `src/14_samples.js`, generated by `tools/gen_samples.py`; layered under the procedural effects, switchable)
- "Space Sound Effects" (SpaceSFX1: explosion1/3/5, laser3/4) by messersm, CC0, https://opengameart.org/content/space-sound-effects (retro/Bfxr character)
- "Synthesized explosion" by qubodup, CC0, https://opengameart.org/content/synthesized-explosion
- "Warp Engine Engage Sound" by bart, CC0, https://opengameart.org/content/warp-engine-engage-sound (first 5 s)
Mapping: boom -> expl1/2/4 (small) or expl3 (large), pulse -> laser3, fang -> laser4 (pitched up), jump -> warp.
Downloaded but not used: "Laser weapon burst fire" (celestialghost8, CC0), "Rocket Engine" (theMinesAreShakin, CC0).

## Options (key O, saved in localStorage `gs_audio`)
Music on/off, music volume, effects volume, sample layer on/off. M still mutes everything. On iOS `audio.volume` is ignored, so the music volume slider may not work there.
