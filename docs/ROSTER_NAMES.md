# Faction roster names (G6 naming pass)

Status: **naming canon for future roster work; no entries in this document add a
ship, a weapon, a statistic, or a mission to the game.** The current playable
roster remains the database and the ship source files. These names give M4 a
consistent vocabulary once the owner chooses which hulls to build.

The player-facing force is still the mixed-origin Colonial Defence Force (CDF).
Its existing Kestrel, Halberd, and Exeter names remain unchanged. The labels
below identify faction production variants, procurement programmes, and planned
signature hulls; they are not retroactive renames.

## Earth-Core Union / UTDF

The ECU's names are institutional and protective: public-service language for
heavy, standardised platforms built around armour, kinetics, and point defence.

| Name | Type / role | One-line identity |
|---|---|---|
| **Kestrel Ward** | line-fighter refit | A UTDF fleet-defence Kestrel package with a conservative kinetic and PD fit. |
| **Merlin Redoubt** | heavy-fighter refit | A deliberately rugged escort fighter for holding a convoy screen. |
| **Aegis-class destroyer** | planned destroyer | A layered-PD escort leader intended to stand between the Halberd and Exeter in a task force. |
| **Lifeline-class SAR cutter** | planned rescue craft | A European Commonwealth-backed recovery cutter built around pods, trauma care, and a magnetic grapple. |
| **Hearthline** | kinetic weapon family | UTDF catalogue name for dependable mass-driver and minigun systems. |
| **Bulwark** | point-defence family | Standard ECU designation for kinetic anti-missile mounts and their control package. |

## Vanderbilt-Astra Conglomerate

VAC products use polished, marketable names: precision, ownership, and advanced
systems rather than brute-force protection. Their hallmark remains licensed
lasers, shields, and electronic warfare.

| Name | Type / role | One-line identity |
|---|---|---|
| **Vesper** | interceptor demonstrator | A fast corporate patrol craft marketed as a sensor-fused contract interceptor. |
| **Specter** | electronic-warfare escort | A disruption platform meant to confuse a threat picture while a client force fires. |
| **Palisade** | shield-support tender | A compact fleet support hull that sells survivability as a subscription service. |
| **Lumen** | beam weapon family | VAC branding for precision laser emitters, including licensed naval installations. |
| **Veil** | electronic-warfare suite | A sensor-jamming and decoy-control package sold with corporate combat support. |

## Void-Born Confederacy

VBC names are short, practical, and drawn from belt work and fast flight. The
line favours high-thrust hulls and autonomous drones because people are harder
to replace than machines.

| Name | Type / role | One-line identity |
|---|---|---|
| **Kite** | light interceptor | A stripped-down belt interceptor built to arrive first and turn inside a larger threat. |
| **Rook** | drone-control striker | A crewed strike craft that coordinates a small, expendable drone element. |
| **Cairn** | mining-convoy escort | A durable outer-system guard ship that doubles as a navigation and salvage node. |
| **SD-4 Sentry** | existing combat drone | The established VBC-designed drone; its name and role are already in game. |
| **Rivet** | compact kinetic family | Field-serviceable light cannons whose parts can be repaired in a habitat workshop. |
| **Tether** | drone-link suite | A resilient command-and-control package for slaved wingmen and autonomous scouts. |

## CDF procurement names

CDF terminology identifies mixed-source packages without pretending the colonies
manufacture every component themselves.

| Name | Type / role | One-line identity |
|---|---|---|
| **Falchion** | planned two-seat heavy fighter | The adopted CDF strike-craft name: two crew and two slaved SD-4 drones in the design brief. |
| **Wayfarer** | evacuation liner programme | A civilian emergency-lift programme for moving habitat populations under escort. |
| **Patchwork** | mixed-origin upgrade standard | A CDF integration label for hulls combining VBC frames, EC subsystems, and whatever the colonies can source. |

## Use rules

- Keep **class**, **variant**, and **product family** distinct in mission copy:
  “Aegis-class destroyer” is a hull; “Hearthline” is a weapon family.
- Do not change an existing in-game name merely to apply this catalogue.
- If an M4 hull is approved, record its actual database id, size, fit, and
  balance evidence separately; this document intentionally supplies none of
  those values.
- Avoid calling any current weapon “acid” in future prose unless the owner
  explicitly restores that concept to the weapon vocabulary.
