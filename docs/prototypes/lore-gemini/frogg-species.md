# FROGG Lore & Species Profile

## 1. Biology & Evolutionary Physiology
- **Classification:** Apex reptilian migratory hive species.
- **Physiological Traits:** Cold-blooded, dense muscular-skeletal structure, scaled lamellar skin, and predatory jaw anatomy.
- **Thermal Dependence:** Being cold-blooded, FROGG biology and technology are intrinsically tied to heat management. Their ships utilize internal plasma heat-sinks and radiative hull channels to regulate both vessel power and bio-neural function.
- **Social Structure:** Migratory swarm networks linked via high-bandwidth bio-psionic and directional laser comms to local Hive Commanders on Carriers (2–3 km) and Motherships (~10 km).

## 2. Philosophical Core: AI Skepticism & "Dead Silicon"
- **Total Rejection of AI:** FROGGs view silicon-based synthetic intelligence with religious and practical revulsion, regarding digital AI as fragile, predictable, and devoid of combat instinct ("dead logic").
- **Wetware Solutions:** In place of combat AI or automated guidance computers, FROGGs utilize bio-neural processing nodes—cloned, streamlined reptilian brain-stems integrated directly into missile seekers, flight control systems, and ship gunnery.
- **Neuro-Slaved Pilots:** FROGG fighters carry no living, conscious pilot in a physical cockpit. Instead, a stripped-down neural cluster is embedded in the airframe, slaved directly to the tactical directives of the sector Hive Commander.
  - *G-Force Tolerance:* With no internal organs, blood vessels, or conscious awareness to disrupt, FROGG fighters pull turn rates that would kill a human pilot (Darter, Leaper, Reaver, Spitter).
  - *No Ejection/Rescue:* Destroying a FROGG fighter permanently destroys its bio-neural node. No rescue, SAR, or bailouts exist for FROGG craft.
  - *Human Contrast:* FROGGs view human reliance on silicon AI drones (`SD-4 Sentry`) as a foolish vulnerability; humans view FROGG neuro-slaving as barbaric bio-mechanics.

## 3. Ship Aesthetic & Biomechanical Design
- **Visual Profile:** Technical biomechanical predators rather than insectoid bugs. Plates resemble dark graphite and gunmetal reptilian hides with rust streaks, swept forward blades, and exposed structural rib arches over the hull.
- **Forward Maw:** Ships feature a wide, open-toothed bow maw reminiscent of an apex marine predator. The inner throat glows with plasma charge lines.
- **Thermal Vents & Seams:** Glowing cyan light seams run between the hull ribs. These are superheated liquid coolant channels that radiate waste heat generated by high-power laser banks (`flaser` and `lance`).
- **Drive Plumes & Acid Weapons:** Quad-blocky aft drives emit cyan plasma plumes. Chemical/acid weapon systems retain a venom-green palette to contrast sharply against the cyan hull illumination.

## 4. Weaponry & Tactical Doctrine
- **Primary Energy:** High-efficiency, instant-hit beam lasers (`flaser` pulse banks for fighters; continuous `lance` beams for Gulper corvettes). Beams require continuous lock-on, ramping up burn damage exponentially over sustained contact.
- **Guided Ordnance:** Advanced bio-neural seeker missiles (`Tick` fast-interceptor missile; `Tadpole` heavy anti-ship missile). High tracking speeds, low susceptibility to human flares/decoys, and long operational ranges.
- **Tactical Feel:** FROGG fleets overwhelm human squadrons through sheer numerical saturation, extreme flight agility, persistent laser burn, and long-range missile suppression.
