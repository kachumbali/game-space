# Historical Background & The Nursery Incident

## 1. The Covert Trigger (The "Oasis-9" Incident)
- **Public Propaganda:** The ECU state media portrays the FROGG invasion as an unprovoked attack by a mindless locust swarm seeking to extinguish humanity.
- **The Classified Reality:** FROGGs are a ancient migratory species that enter multi-century deep-space dormancy inside dense asteroid clusters while their Hive Motherships regenerate power cores.
- **The Vanderbilt-Astra Intrusion:** Fifteen years ago, a corporate mining fleet operated by the Vanderbilt-Astra Conglomerate breached an unmapped planetoid in Sector Oasis-9. Inside, they discovered a dormant FROGG **Bio-Anchor Nursery** containing millions of sleeping bio-neural units and three dormant Hive Motherships.
- **The Awakening:** VAC attempted to secretly strip-mine the dormant motherships for bio-composite hull plating and exotic plasma capacitors. The forced extraction severed a primary neural node, triggering an interstellar distress signal that re-routed the main FROGG Hive Fleet directly toward human space.

## 2. Engagement Limits & Strategic Bottlenecks
- **Jump Drive Limitation:** Hyper-jump drives require massive power grids and spatial stabilizers. Only capital ships (500m Frigate class and larger) can execute jumps.
- **Carrier Dependence:** All fighters, corvettes, and sub-capital craft must dock inside a Frigate, Carrier, or Spun Asteroid axial bay prior to jump initiation.
- **Combat Consequence:** If a human Frigate's jump drive is destroyed or forced into an emergency retreat, all deployed fighters and drone wings are marooned in the combat sector.
