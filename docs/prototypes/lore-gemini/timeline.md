# Historical Timeline & 2180s Geopolitical Order

## 1. Timeline of Expansion
- **2030s–2060s (The Great Inundation):** Severe climate devastation, sea-level rise, and agricultural collapses destabilize Earth. The global population drops and stabilizes at roughly 8 billion through enforced geo-engineering and severe resource rationing.
- **2070s (The Fold Breakthrough):** Development of the sub-space Jump Drive. The technology requires a massive spatial-displacement envelope—establishing the physical rule that **only hulls 500m or larger can stabilize a jump field**.
- **2080s–2140s (The Great Re-Seeding):** Humanity expands aggressively into surrounding star systems. Mega-corporations and planetary national blocs sponsor massive 2km Mega-Transports carrying hundreds of thousands of colonists in deep cryo-stasis to hollowed-out asteroid hubs and outpost worlds.
- **2180s (Present Day):** Human space spans a 30-light-year bubble. Earth is unified under a powerful United Nations administration, but deep-space colonies are caught between national bloc rivalries, corporate monopolies, and the emerging FROGG threat.
